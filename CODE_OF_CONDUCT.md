# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and operators with respect. Discuss the work, not the person.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private
information, and publication of credentials or engagement evidence are not
accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with attention to the safety and privacy of the
people involved. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the project and its community.
